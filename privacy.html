Lumen · Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · 萤传 / Lumen for iOS · 中文版

1. Scope of This Policy

This privacy policy applies only to the Lumen iOS app (“the app”). The app is an offline file receiver that decodes animated color codes captured by the camera, on the device, using the libcimbar protocol.

2. What Information We Collect

The app does not collect any of your personal information, including but not limited to:

  • Identity information such as name, phone number, email, ID number or address
  • Location, device IMEI or other unique identifiers
  • Sensitive data such as contacts, photos or microphone input
  • The content of files you receive

The app does not upload any data to any server and does not share data with third parties.

3. What Permissions We Use

  • Camera (required): used only to scan the sender's animated color code, and only while the Scan tab is active. Frames are decoded in memory on the device and are never stored or uploaded.
  • Add to Photos (optional): used only when you choose to save a received photo or video to your library, or turn on “Auto-Save to Photos”. The app never reads anything in your library.

4. How Files Are Stored

Received files are saved in the app's Documents folder (Documents/CimbarTransfer/Inbox). These files:

  • Stay on your device only and are never uploaded or synced to any cloud
  • Can be browsed in the Files app
  • Can be deleted in the app at any time, or deleted automatically under Settings → Auto-Delete
  • Are deleted together with the app when you delete it

5. Security Notice

The app decodes using the open-source libcimbar project (Mozilla Public License 2.0). The transfer is a public visual encoding and is not encrypted:

  • The animated code is fully visible on screen; people nearby can photograph, record or capture it, even from a distance
  • Do not use it to transfer sensitive content such as trade secrets or private personal files
  • If you must send a sensitive file, put it in an archive encrypted with a strong password first

6. Children's Privacy

The app is rated for ages 4 and up and does not collect any information from minors. If you believe information was collected by mistake, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. We will announce significant changes prominently in the app. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, tell us through Report a Problem in About Lumen, which opens github.com/Brulek/libcimbar/issues.

If the English and Chinese versions differ, the Chinese version prevails.

萤传 · 隐私政策

最后更新时间：2026 年 10 月 · 法律文本以中文版本为准

一、本政策的适用范围

本隐私政策仅适用于「萤传」iOS 客户端（以下简称「本应用」）。本应用是一款离线的文件接收工具，使用 libcimbar 协议在本机解码相机捕获到的彩色动画码。

二、我们收集哪些信息

本应用不收集您的任何个人信息，包括但不限于：

  • 姓名、电话、邮箱、身份证号、地址等身份信息
  • 位置信息、设备 IMEI 或唯一标识
  • 通讯录、相册、麦克风等敏感数据
  • 您接收到的文件内容

本应用不会将任何数据上传至任何服务器，也不与第三方共享。

三、我们使用哪些权限

  • 相机权限（必需）：仅用于扫描发送端的彩色动画码。该权限只在「扫码」页激活时使用，画面仅在本机内存中实时解码，不会被存储或上传。
  • 相册写入权限（可选）：仅在您主动选择，或开启「照片和视频自动存到相册」后，将接收到的图片和视频保存到相册时使用。本应用不读取您相册中的任何内容。

四、文件如何存储

接收到的文件保存在本应用的「文档」目录（Documents/CimbarTransfer/Inbox）。这些文件：

  • 仅保存在您本机设备上，不会被上传或同步到任何云端
  • 可在系统「文件」App 中浏览
  • 您可随时在应用内手动删除，或在「设置 → 自动清理」中设置自动删除
  • 卸载本应用时，这些文件会随应用一并删除

五、本应用的安全声明

本应用基于 libcimbar 开源项目（Mozilla Public License 2.0）实现解码。其传输方式本质上是公开的视觉编码，不进行加密：

  • 彩色动画码在屏幕上完整可见，附近的人可拍摄、录屏甚至远距离捕获
  • 请勿用于传输商业机密、个人隐私文件等敏感内容
  • 如必须传输敏感文件，请先用强密码加密压缩后再发送

六、未成年人保护

本应用面向 4 岁以上用户，不针对未成年人收集任何信息。如发现误收集请联系我们删除。

七、政策的变更

本政策可能不时更新。重大变更将在应用内显著位置告知您。继续使用本应用即视为您同意更新后的政策。

八、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请在「关于萤传」里点「反馈问题」（github.com/Brulek/libcimbar/issues）告诉我们。
